DJ Sinistor

Primitive garage. Dark fuzz.

Instagram Facebook

DJ Sinistor spins primitive garage sounds dragged from the shadows of dusty basements, forgotten dance halls and smoke-filled back rooms.

Get ready for raw fuzz guitars, haunted Farfisa organs, pounding rhythms and wild 7" vinyl selections moving through the darker corners of garage rock, freakbeat, rhythm & blues, proto-punk and exotic sounds from the 1960s and beyond.

Inspired by the savage energy of bands like The Sonics, The Seeds, The Music Machine, Link Wray, The Haunted, The Count Five, The Mourning Reign but also the more "modern" bands like The Cramps and The Fuzztones, DJ Sinistor blends primitive stompers, fuzz-drenched dancers and eerie movers into all-night rituals of rhythm and reverb.

From Back From The Grave, Pebbles and Nuggets classics to obscure killers hidden deep inside forgotten compilations, the sound moves between snarling garage punk, hypnotic go-go beats, jungle rhythms, trashy rock'n'roll and shadowy horror atmospheres.

Expect songs like “Psychotic Reaction”, “99th Floor”, “No Friend of Mine”, “The Witch”, “Jack The Ripper”, “Human Fly” and countless obscure 45s built for dim lights, sweat, movement and late-night delirium.
